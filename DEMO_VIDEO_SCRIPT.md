## 🎬 Recording Tips

### Screen Recording
- Use OBS Studio or Loom
- 1920x1080 resolution
- 60 FPS for smooth animations
- Hide cursor during transitions

### Voiceover
- Use professional mic (Blue Yeti, Rode NT-USB)
- Record in quiet room
- Speak clearly, moderate pace
- Add subtle reverb in post

### Editing
- Use DaVinci Resolve or Adobe Premiere
- Add smooth transitions (0.5s fade)
- Background music at -20dB
- Color grade for consistency
- Add subtle zoom effects on key features

### Music
- Epidemic Sound: "Tech Upbeat"
- Artlist: "Innovation"
- YouTube Audio Library: "Inspired"
